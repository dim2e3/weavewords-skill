# English A2 → B1 — course plan

<!-- Sample plan. /plan-lessons replaces it with one built for your profile. -->

Each lesson has one grammar focus and 8–12 words or phrases. Run it with `/lesson N`.

| # | Topic | Grammar focus | Vocabulary (8–12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning routine | Present Simple, adverbs of frequency | wake up, have breakfast, commute, usually, rarely, rush, alarm, get ready, on time, late |
| 2 | At the café | Countable/uncountable, some/any | order, bill, tip, a slice of, a cup of, refill, takeaway, reserve, menu, sparkling |
| 3 | Weekend plans | be going to vs will | plan, book, look forward to, sightseeing, day off, stay in, go out, invite, cancel |
| 4 | Travel problems | Past Simple (regular/irregular) | miss a flight, delay, lose luggage, check in, boarding pass, gate, connection, refund, complain |
| 5 | Describing people | Adjective order, comparatives | outgoing, reliable, shy, curly, tall, in his thirties, look like, take after, get on with |
| 6 | Work and studies | Present Perfect (ever/never/just) | apply for, deadline, colleague, manager, promotion, meeting, part-time, experience, skill |
| 7 | Health | should / shouldn't | catch a cold, feel dizzy, prescription, painkiller, appointment, rest, recover, symptoms |
| 8 | Shopping | Too / enough, sizes | fit, suit, try on, receipt, discount, sale, refund, exchange, afford, bargain |
| 9 | Home and chores | have to / don't have to | do the dishes, vacuum, tidy up, laundry, rent, landlord, flatmate, bills, fix |
| 10 | Technology | Present Continuous for now/trends | charge, update, download, sign up, log in, password, screen, battery, app, scroll |
| 11 | Food and recipes | Imperatives, sequencing words | chop, boil, fry, stir, add, ingredient, recipe, first, then, finally |
| 12 | Past experiences | Past Simple vs Present Perfect | ever, already, yet, once, a long time ago, memorable, abroad, adventure, regret |
| 13 | City life | There is/are, prepositions of place | crowded, quiet, neighbourhood, nearby, opposite, next to, public transport, rush hour |
| 14 | Feelings | -ed / -ing adjectives | bored, boring, excited, exciting, tired, tiring, worried, relieved, embarrassed |
| 15 | Future goals | will / might / hope to | achieve, improve, goal, career, save up, move abroad, learn, someday, probably |
| 16 | Review & speaking | Mixed | words from lessons 1–15 that are still weak in Weavewords |
