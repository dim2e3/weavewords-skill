# /speak

Follow `method/lesson-method.md` → sections 7–8. Unlike the app, where speaking isn't graded, here you **coach** it.

1. Read `profile.md`. Pick 4–6 target expressions: from the topic, the last lesson, or `get_due_words` (Pro).
2. **Situation round** (2 situations): describe a realistic situation at the learner's level and ask for 3–5 sentences. In the Claude or ChatGPT apps they can answer by voice. Keep `useful_language` hidden unless they ask.
3. After each answer:
   - ✓ list the target expressions they used (the goal is ≥3);
   - fix the 2–3 most important errors (`❌ … ✅ …`);
   - offer a more natural version of their answer (same ideas, better phrasing);
   - mention one phrase worth stealing.
4. **Rapid-fire round** (3–4 prompts): one-sentence answers, each with a one-line correction.
5. Collect the new phrases that came up and offer `/save-words`. Log one line to `progress.md`, including any recurring errors.
