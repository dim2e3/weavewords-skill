# /story

1. Read `profile.md` and `progress.md`. Gather 6–10 of the learner's words:
   - `list_words` sort=recent (limit 20);
   - weak words from `progress.md`;
   - with Pro, `list_words` sort=hardest (limit 10).

   Prefer words they're still learning.
2. Write a **150–250 word** story or dialogue at their CEFR level on the topic (or one of their interests). Use each chosen word naturally at least once and **bold** them. New words above their level: at most 3, each glossed in brackets in the native language.
3. Ask **3–5 comprehension questions**, one per message: 2 about content, 1 about inference, and 1–2 "what does ‹bold word› mean here?".
4. Give feedback after each answer. At the end, offer:
   - `/save-words` for the glossed new words;
   - retelling the story in their own words, which you coach like `/speak`.
5. Log one line to `progress.md`.
